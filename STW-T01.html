STW‑T01 · Light Multirole Transport
STINGWASP

A compact independent transport built around wasp-inspired industrial biomimicry: a pointed forward head, layered armour, broad cargo abdomen, compact crew spaces and a machinery-heavy aft section. The ship deliberately has no wings. The wasp identity comes from proportion, segmentation, stance and clustered exterior systems.

Canonical design rule: the StingWasp is one physical ship. Exterior, decks, compartments, cargo volume, airlocks, landing gear and machinery must agree spatially.
